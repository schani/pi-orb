## Findings

- **#2 product:** serial polling blocked orb B while A held health/claim/delivery/history/commit/naming or sustained catch-up. The first history red and seven pre-fix replays timed out at `b-first receipt before A release`. Independent per-orb central/history/naming lanes fix later-tick starvation; joining a concurrent sweep would not. The old scenario is retained because its two-task trace cannot replay the worker-mailbox topology.
- **#2 fixture-only:** intermediate same-orb inputs sat behind deliberately blocked FIFO receipts (fixture now leaves FIFO clear); shutdown fixtures first used a nonexistent control port, then an untriggered naming crash. Corrected fixtures preceded product edits.
- **#2 shutdown product:** sustained nonempty history masked shutdown/fatal supervision. Pre-fix replays timed out at `sustained source must not block drain`. Stop/crash now yields between rounds after admitted I/O's safe commit, without clearing retry/cadence or inventing caught-up/failure. Strict post-fix replay diverges at 108/122 because the next catch-up round was removed: not a green replay. Fresh schedules validate the invariants. Archive `2/shutdown/receipt.md` gives reproduction on independent-lane audit baselines, not main.
- **#4 product:** cross-critical coalescing delivered B's compaction before A's finish, leaving B busy but not compacting. All critical frames now fence all transient kinds. First red and pre-fix replay reproduced; corrected replay passes (seven drain prefixes × 80 schedules).
- **#5 product:** repeated retries shared ownership, yielding 3 publications/3 timers/2 after close instead of 1/1/0. Retry advances generation; publication rechecks ownership after synchronous callbacks. All six pre-fix replays failed; corrected replays pass (six cases × 30 schedules).
- Unexpected poll crashes emit one sanitized `poll-task-crashed` edge. No persisted-state change or healthy-poll logs.

## Rules

- Polling uses independent per-orb lanes; a held or catching-up orb must not delay other orbs or later ticks.
- Shutdown/fatal supervision yields between history rounds after admitted I/O commits.
- Critical outbound frames fence all transient coalescing.
- A retry advances its ownership generation; publication rechecks ownership after synchronous callbacks.
