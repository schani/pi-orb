- **Delete retired signing keys past the JWKS overlap window** (`docs/workload-identity.md` stage 2; `apps/control-plane/src/domain/signing-keys.ts` and `assembleJwks`'s header both defer the work to a TODO item that did not exist until 2026-08-22). So every rotation permanently accumulates one row and one live private key that nothing will ever use and every future compromise of the secret can still reach. The retention decision was deliberate for stage 2 (dropping a public key too early breaks live tokens; publishing a spare is harmless), but the cleanup half was only ever deferred.
