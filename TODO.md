- **Delete retired signing keys past the JWKS overlap window** (`docs/workload-identity.md` stage 2; `apps/control-plane/src/domain/signing-keys.ts` and `assembleJwks`'s header both defer the work to a TODO item that did not exist until 2026-08-22). So every rotation permanently accumulates one row and one live private key that nothing will ever use and every future compromise of the secret can still reach.
