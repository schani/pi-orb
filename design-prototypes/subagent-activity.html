SUBAGENTS / TWO INDEPENDENT DESIGN CHOICES

Interactive study · simulated data only · no changes to running orbs

Open the notice rows and the live count. Inspect a child; use Escape or the close control to dismiss an inspector. “Advance one child” exercises count changes without timers or runtime requests.

Queued and finishing children are not counted as running. Unknown is “—”, not zero. Known-empty indicators disappear. The numeric count appears in one product location at a time.

The sample conversation is abbreviated. These proposals do not suppress or combine the parent’s acknowledgement messages, or change notification delivery. No child transcripts, retry buttons, or child-only Stop controls are introduced.
